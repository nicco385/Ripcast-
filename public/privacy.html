Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Choc's Spot (formerly Ripcast)

Choc's Spot is built to stay private. There are no accounts, ads or trackers.

What stays on your phone

Your catch log, favourites and any photos you add are stored only on this device. We never see them and they are not uploaded.

Location

If you allow location, it is used on your phone to find nearby spots and to request weather for that place. Coordinates go to Open-Meteo (the weather provider) so they can return the forecast. We do not collect, store or sell your location.

Weather and map data

Weather and marine data come from Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Map tiles and map data are © OpenStreetMap contributors, shown with Leaflet.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-in or account
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal information

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the Choc's Spot GitHub repo.

Scores, tides and spot picks are a guide only. Always check BOM warnings and Victorian Fisheries Authority rules, and fish safely.
